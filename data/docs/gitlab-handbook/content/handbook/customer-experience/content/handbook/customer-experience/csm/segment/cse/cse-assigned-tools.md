title: "CSE Assigned: Tools & Resources"
description: "Tools and resources CSEs use to operate the CSE Assigned motion."
---

Everything you need to operate as a CSE Assigned.

- **Gainsight**: health assessments in the Timeline. CTAs for follow-up work. Do not create a CTA for every health assessment; the assessment lives in Timeline.
- **Salesforce**: "Scale CSE" field on Account and Opportunity shows the assigned CSE. Primary source for assignment visibility across the team.
- **Slack `#ask-cse-coverage`**: questions, coverage gaps, and edge cases. Post here before escalating elsewhere.
- **Customer Intro Email Template**: used by AEs and RMs to introduce the CSE to assigned accounts.
- **[CSE Operating Rhythm - engagement request process](/handbook/customer-experience/csm/segment/cse/cse-operating-rhythm/#success-on-demand-cse-engagement-request-process)**: how to request a CSE engagement or open a case for an assigned account.
- **[Internal Briefing slide deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1EN7ga7XF1Yxi5w7277rem_EE_Zb2X3m2dHb6iFIPTgk/edit?slide=id.p1#slide=id.p1)**: to present the motion to internal teams or individual team members.
- **[Customer Facing slide deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1R5tgskgNzfRlYBj3uFEjf-dsaig7fiGqJh3gomoYMRo/)**: to support your intro meetings with customers.
