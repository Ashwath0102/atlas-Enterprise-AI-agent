Customer interactions and motion differentiation.

## Customer engagement

CSEs engage customers directly when their work requires it, to validate signals, gather context not available in product data, or advance a risk or enablement action, with the account team kept in the loop. The CSE's health assessment adds to what the account team already knows.

An **engagement** is a bounded, topic-scoped body of work with a customer, for example answering a technical question, working through an adoption topic, or resolving a technical issue (but not a break/fix situation, this is for the support team). It typically takes 1-3 interactions (calls, emails, or working sessions), not an ongoing relationship. This is distinct from the mandatory quarterly customer touch below: a touch keeps the account warm, an engagement is a discrete piece of work. CSE Assigned accounts are capped at **three engagements per account per quarter**.
CSEs should not join persistent Slack channels that include customers. Engagements are bounded and topic-scoped by design. A standing customer Slack channel creates an unbounded, always-on relationship that works against that model and blurs the line with Success Tiers coverage. Use email or scheduled calls instead.

> **When a customer does not engage:** the health assessment still proceeds using available data (usage signals, Gainsight history, AE/SA context). Outreach attempts are logged, sentiment is captured, and "lack of customer engagement" is used as the CTA closure reason where applicable. A customer's failure to engage is itself a risk signal in the health assessment.

## CSE Assigned vs. Success Tiers

CSE Assigned and Success Tiers are complementary, not competing. CSE Assigned is a risk-focused, quarterly motion included in the customer's contract. Success Tiers are a paid, high-touch offering. When a customer requests more depth than CSE Assigned delivers, that is a Success Tiers opportunity for the AE, not a scope expansion for the CSE.

| | Pool (CSE) | CSE Assigned | Success Tiers (Essentials / Advanced / Signature) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer eligibility** | Below $100K, no ST | $100K+, no ST (no ceiling; bounded by CSE capacity) | $100K+ (paid) |
| **Engagement model** | Reactive, case-based | Proactive, quarterly | Proactive, ongoing |
| **Named resource** | - | ✔ | ✔ |
| **Cadence** | On demand | Quarterly | Monthly or more |
| **Engagement limit** | 3 cases max per half | 3 engagements per account per quarter | Uncapped (per standard cadence) |
| **Health monitoring** | - | ✔ | ✔ |
| **Escalation management** | - | ✔ | ✔ |
| **Success planning** | - | - | ✔ |
| **EBR / QBR** | - | - | ✔ |
| **Workshops** | Public schedule only | Public schedule only | Tailored |
| **Cost to customer** | Included | Included | Paid |
