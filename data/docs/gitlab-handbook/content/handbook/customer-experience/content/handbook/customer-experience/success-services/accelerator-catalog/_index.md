Accelerators are 12-week structured enablement programs delivered by your [Customer Success Architect (CSA)](/handbook/customer-experience/csm/segment/csa/), aligned to your mutual success plan and strategic goals. They are included with GitLab's **Advanced** (2/year) and **Signature** (4/year) [Success Tiers](/handbook/customer-experience/success-services/).

For the full interactive marketplace, visit the [GitLab Accelerator Marketplace](https://cloud.gitlab-accelerator-marketplace.com/available_products).

## What Is an Accelerator?

An Accelerator is a customized form of enablement that a GitLab CSA defines and delivers in partnership with your team. Each Accelerator:

- Runs over approximately **12 weeks**
- Is scoped to your specific environment, goals, and maturity level
- Follows a four-phase lifecycle: **Discovery → Design → Deliver → Measure**
- Produces measurable outcomes tracked in a shared GitLab collaboration project

> Customer Success is not permitted to be "hands on keyboard" with customers. CSAs provide best practices, architectural guidance, and tailored adoption plans. If a customer needs GitLab to actively implement or configure their environment, that work is handled by [Professional Services](/handbook/customer-experience/professional-services-engineering/).

## Accelerator Lifecycle

| Phase | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Discovery** | Assess current state, understand business objectives, and define desired outcomes |
| **Design** | Build a customized enablement plan with milestones and a backlog of tracked issues |
| **Deliver** | Execute through workshops, hands-on sessions, and async guidance over ~10 weeks |
| **Measure** | Assess results against defined outcomes, document impact, and plan the next Accelerator |

## Available Accelerators

### CI/CD

| Accelerator | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Introduction to CI/CD** | Getting-started accelerator covering GitLab CI architecture, `gitlab-ci.yml` basics, and common pipeline patterns for teams new to the platform |
| **Runner Fleet Guidance** | Optimize and manage a GitLab Runner fleet at scale through maturity assessment, informational sessions, and interactive training |
| **Rules and Pipeline Flow** | Advanced concepts covering pipeline flow control using rules, DAG, parent/child pipelines, and optimization methods |
| **CI Templates and Reusability** | Creation and use of CI templates and workflow optimization for standardized, reusable pipelines |
| **Product Coach (CI/CD)** | Deep-dive consultative guidance on pipeline best practices, producing a detailed guidance report across 20+ optimization categories |
| **Pipeline Template Optimization** | 12-week program developing standardized, reusable templates tailored to your project types, languages, and compliance requirements |

## How to Get an Accelerator

Accelerators are included with GitLab's **Advanced** and **Signature** Success Tiers:

- **Advanced tier**: up to 2 Accelerators per year
- **Signature tier**: up to 4 Accelerators per year

To get started:

1. Visit [about.gitlab.com/services/](https://about.gitlab.com/services/) to learn about Success Tiers
2. Purchase the Advanced or Signature tier
3. Work with your assigned [CSA](/handbook/customer-experience/csm/segment/csa/) to select and scope your first Accelerator

For the full interactive catalog, visit the [GitLab Accelerator Marketplace](https://cloud.gitlab-accelerator-marketplace.com/available_products).
