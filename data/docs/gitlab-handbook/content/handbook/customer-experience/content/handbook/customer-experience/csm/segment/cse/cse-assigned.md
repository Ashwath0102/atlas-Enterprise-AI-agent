Customer Success Engineering · Proactive Motion

CSE Assigned is a proactive motion in which a named Customer Success Engineer owns recurring health assessments and risk management for a defined book of accounts. It is one of three CSE motions, designed to close the retention gap for accounts that don't have a named CSM/CSA, by ensuring every qualifying account has a dedicated technical owner without requiring a case to be opened.

Each CSE owns a book of approximately 50 accounts, conducting recurring health assessments that evaluate product adoption, technical risk, and customer health. The CSE works as a technical extension of the account team, surfacing risk signals, managing escalations, and flagging growth opportunities, without requiring a case or ticket from the customer.

This motion sits between the **Pool** motion (reactive, case-based coverage) and **[Success Tiers](/handbook/customer-experience/success-services/)** (paid, high-touch engagements). CSE Assigned is included in the customer's contract at no additional cost. It is not a replacement for Success Tiers. When a customer requests deeper engagement, that's a Success Tiers opportunity for the AE.

> **Eligibility:** CSE Assigned covers qualifying accounts without a CSM or CSA. For eligibility threshold, consult the internal handbook. The practical limit is capacity: each CSE carries roughly 50 accounts, so coverage is bounded by book size.
