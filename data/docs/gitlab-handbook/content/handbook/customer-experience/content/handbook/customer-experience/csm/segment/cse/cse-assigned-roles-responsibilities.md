This page formalizes the RACI proposed in [work item #24 — Clarify roles and engagement limits for CSE assigned coverage](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/customer-success/csmerm/customer-success-engineering/cse-assigned/-/work_items/24), scoped specifically to CSE Assigned. It does not change or replace the general [CSE DCI matrix](/handbook/customer-experience/csm/segment/cse/#cse-operating-rhythm) on the CSE homepage, which continues to apply as-is.

> What follows is current guidance for CSE Assigned, only represents the CSE team point of view, and is not a rigid or final model. Real accounts don't always fit neatly into a scope table. CSEs, managers, and the account team should stay flexible and adapt to whatever solution best serves the customer, rather than treating this page as a hard boundary. 

## Working principles

- RM and AE retain commercial ownership and renewal orchestration.
- SAs remain key partners for pre-sales continuity, opportunity context, account strategy, and technical input in support of growth motions.
- Assigned CSE owns proactive technical signal review, targeted technical engagement, and technical escalation leadership.
- DAP credit consumption is a shared responsibility between CSEs and SAs: CSEs help identify adoption/consumption patterns in-life; SAs help align usage to the right solutioning, value path, and expansion context.
- A need for recurring strategic cadence, executive alignment, success planning, or broad stakeholder orchestration signals **named CSMA coverage**, not expanded assigned CSE scope.
- A need for scoped delivery work (migration execution, implementation, architecture packages, custom integration delivery) signals **PS scope**, not expanded assigned CSE scope.

## RACI: customers with assigned CSE coverage

*R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed*

| Responsibility | CSE | CSE mgmt | RM | AE | SA | PS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identify technical risk and adoption signals | R | A | C | I | C | I |
| Maintain proactive technical health view for assigned accounts | R | A | C | I | C | I |
| Document findings in CTA / timeline / internal notes | A/R | C | I | I | I | I |
| Prioritize which assigned accounts need action next | R | A | C | I | C | I |
| Coordinate internal account alignment before customer outreach | R | C | A | C | C | I |
| Request warm intro / validate account context before proactive engagement | R | I | A | C | C | I |
| Run discrete technical discovery, adoption, troubleshooting, or optimization sessions | A/R | C | C | I | C | I |
| Lead technical escalation handling for assigned accounts | A/R | C | I | C | C | I |
| Review DAP credit consumption and identify adoption/value gaps | R | C | I | I | R | I |
| Identify need for named CSMA coverage | R | C | C | A | C | I |
| Identify and qualify PS opportunities | R | I | C | R | C | C |
| Own renewal strategy and commercial packaging | I | I | R | A | C | C |
| Own executive alignment, success planning, recurring business cadence | I | I | C | I | C | I |
| Approve exceptions to standard scope or high-touch asks | R | A | C | C | C | C |

## Engagement limits: CSE pool vs. CSE assigned vs. CSMAs

Assigned CSE coverage does **not** become named CSMA coverage: no default cadence calls, no EBRs/QBRs, no custom workshops, no unlimited engagement model.

| Action | CSE pool | CSE assigned | CSMA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Respond to inbound help requests | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Provide reactive technical guidance (bounded) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Proactively monitor a defined book for technical risk/opportunity | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Produce recurring internal health updates | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ (account planning, not CSE-style HAs) |
| Act as named technical DRI for escalations | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Own a standing customer-facing technical relationship by default | ❌ | ✅ (limited, within assigned model) | ✅ |
| Engagement limit | 3 cases max per half | 3 engagements per account per quarter | Uncapped (per standard cadence) |
| Hold recurring cadence calls by default | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Run EBRs / QBRs | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Own success plan / roadmap governance / stakeholder orchestration | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Deliver custom workshops outside bounded engagements | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ (delivery may still sit with PS/specialist) |
| Continue unlimited follow-up after initial asks resolved | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ (where named coverage applies) |
| Identify PS / Success Tier expansion signals | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Deliver project-style implementation work | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ (moves to PS) |
| Deliver migration execution / custom integration / architecture package | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ (moves to PS) |
| Own renewal relationship and commercials | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ (remains RM/AE) |

## Practical boundary triggers

**Keep with assigned CSE:**

- Customer needs a targeted technical conversation.
- Customer has adoption friction, configuration issues, or product questions.
- There is a technical escalation where continuity matters.
- Health review shows a concrete technical risk that can be addressed in a bounded engagement.

**Move toward named CSMA coverage:**

- Customer expects recurring cadence and structured follow-up.
- Customer needs broad stakeholder alignment across business and technical teams.
- Customer wants roadmap governance, success planning, or recurring executive review.
- Customer behavior suggests ongoing high-touch coordination rather than discrete technical interventions.

**Move toward PS:**

- Customer needs migration or consolidation execution.
- Customer needs architecture design packaged as delivery work.
- Customer needs custom integration design/build support.
- Customer needs a large rollout with milestones, project structure, and implementation ownership.
