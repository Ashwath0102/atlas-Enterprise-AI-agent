## GitLab Revenue Academy

The GitLab Revenue Academy is the onboarding program for all new hires in the CRO org. It is a blended learning experience combining self-paced eLearning, live virtual workshops, and an in-person graduation event, designed to get new field team members customer-ready within their first 30 days.

Attendance at all program components is mandatory. If you need to miss a session for any reason, please obtain approval from your Regional Director in writing.

For full program details, current cohort information, and role-based onboarding resources, visit the [Revenue Academy Handbook Page](/handbook/sales/onboarding/).
