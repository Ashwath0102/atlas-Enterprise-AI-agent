## Linking knowledge and other content to support tickets

**Are you linking content to a support ticket?**

Start with SEARCHING for knowledge!

In Zendesk, within a ticket click on the Knowledge Icon in the Right panel, and then use the Search (magnifying glass) to search for a resolution to that ticket. You can SEARCH for documentation and/or knowledge articles within that search functionality!  

When you find knowledge (Documentation or article) you can and shold then LINK taht knowledge to the support ticket. Linking knowledge to a support ticket creates a critical feedback loop for us. It provides valuable insights into opportunities, as well as measurements.  

Linking knowledge helps us identify:

1. Where we have product gaps
1. Where knowledge is strong
1. Where knowledge is missing
1. Where demand is changing
1. Where the same problems keep recurring

![KCS Search](/images/support/assets/kcs.png)

**How can YOU help?**

We have the capability to link knowledge throughout the lifecycle of the ticket (not just at resolution), however we should make sure we link before resolving the ticket completely.
Note: Not **EVERY** ticket will require a link. Do not link just for the sake of linking. Link knowledge when it adds value, not just to check a box. It should be helpful!

**How do I link**

- Use the Knowledge Capture app (on the right side when you are in a support ticket).
- Use **Search** to search for an article or a document.
- You can view the article in the ticket view or you can view it on the page view.
- Use the link icon to link the article or document to the support ticket.

**When to link:**

- When an article, document or issue is used to solve the problem.
- It benefits the customer.
- It helped in your research for the support ticket.
- It provides knowledge for you and the customer.

**When NOT to link:**

- The article doesn't cover anything related to the support ticket.
- The article wasn't used to help resolve the ticket.

Link content when there is real value in the connection. The goal is to build intelligence around support tickets - e.g. what documentation helps, where gaps exist, what could have been deflected.
