## Emergency Requests for Zendesk U.S. Government

Our U.S. Government Customers will be provided with an email address they can email to in case of emergency. The emergency email triggers an emergency page that our engineers will respond to within the time outlined in our Support Page.

## Working Tickets in Zendesk U.S. Government

Please see [Working with U.S. Government Support tickets](/handbook/support/workflows/usgovernment_tickets/) for information about working tickets on this instance.
